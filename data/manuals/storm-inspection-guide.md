# Storm Inspection Guide

## Page 1

Storm response begins with scene safety. Treat every downed or visibly damaged conductor as energized until qualified personnel verify an isolation. Establish a safe perimeter before approaching the asset, and record the incident location, time, weather conditions, and reporting technician.

## Page 2

For a leaning wooden pole, inspect the foundation, guy wires, crossarm attachment points, insulators, conductor clearance, and nearby vegetation. Photograph each damaged component and record whether the transformer, fuse, and protective equipment appear secure.

## Page 3

For a split or fractured crossarm after high winds, keep the line out of service until a qualified crew evaluates the conductor tension and hardware. The restoration plan should identify the replacement crossarm, required equipment, traffic-control needs, and any downstream customers affected.

## Page 4

Escalate an incident immediately when a conductor is down near a public road, school, hospital, fire, or active work zone. If visual or telemetry evidence is ambiguous, mark the incident as insufficient evidence and request a daylight photograph or physical inspection rather than guessing.
